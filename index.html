AdventureWorks 2025: end-to-end customer analytics

A customer analytics platform built on Microsoft's AdventureWorks database. I designed a Bronze, Silver and Gold warehouse in SQL Server, loaded it with a metadata-driven SSIS package, modelled it in Power BI, and fitted BG/NBD and Gamma-Gamma models in Python whose predictions are written back to the warehouse.

The part I'm proudest of: I tested the models against simple baselines instead of trusting the totals, found where they failed, and documented why.

How I work

The same order in every project, because a dashboard is only as good as the question and the model underneath it.

  1. Business question

    Write down the decision first. In AdventureWorks: who to retain, who is slipping, what customers will do next.

  2. Data engineering

    Layered warehouse, repeatable loads, a validation query at every stage.

  3. Modelling

    Star schema and one semantic model, so every report uses the same definitions.

  4. Analysis and prediction

    RFM, cohorts, CLV, BTYD. Every model is checked against a simple baseline.

  5. Decision support

    Each segment ends in an action, a target KPI and a list of who to contact.
